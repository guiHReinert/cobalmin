#### [English for Noobs - PDF](https://englishfornoobs.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/11/Comparison-as-as-Exercises-with-answers-.pdf)

**Exercise 1**: Build sentences with the comparative of equality "as + adj + as" (sentences can be negative!):

**Ex**: Kevin weighs 80kg. Max weighs 80kg. (is/heavy) → Kevin is as heavy as Max.

1) The red house is 50 years old. The green house is 50 years old. (is/old)
	→ The red house *is as old as the* the green house.
2) Sam did not do well in the English test. Julia did better in the English test. (do/well)
	→ Sam *didn't do as well as* Julia.
3) The impala runs 90km per hour. The cheetah runs 120km per hour. (run/fast)
	→ The impala *doesn't run as fast as* the cheetah.
4) The first exam was difficult. The second exam was difficult too. (was/difficult)
	→ The first exam *was as difficult as* the second exam.
5) Roubaix is not very beautiful. Lyon is more beautiful. (is/beautiful)
	→ Roubaix *is not as beautiful as* Lyon.
6) Tina is 1.60cms tall. Sarah is 1.60cms tall. (is/tall)
	→ Tina *is as tall as* Sarah.
7) Italy is 35ºC in summer. Croatia is 35ºC in summer. (is/hot)
	→ Italy *is as hot as* Croatia in summer.
8) Paul is not very patient. Claire is more patient. (is/patient)
	→ Paul *is not as patient as* Claire.
9) My dad's face was red. A tomato is red. (was/red)
	→ My dad's face *was as red as* a tomato.
10) Kevin talks very quietly. Tom talks more loudly. (talk/loudly)
	-> Kevin *doesn't talk as loudly as* Tom.

#### [Comparative as... as with nouns](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1nkWeQVtrbhlpqQLIsJsRuKFy7-4hF5JiuT-a-PmfiUg/edit?usp=sharing)

1\) Complete the statements with “many” before count nouns and “much” before noncount nouns:

- There aren’t as *many* stores and supermarkets in this neighborhood.
- The suburbs don’t have as *much* traffic as downtown.
- The rural area doesn’t have as *much* phone reception.
- The apartment where I live doesn’t get as *much* light as the house I just bought.
- In the suburbs, there isn’t as *much* pollution as the industrial district because there aren’t as *many* industries.
- I can’t decide: the first apartment doesn’t have as *much* closet space, but the second one doesn’t have as *many* bathrooms.
- The house has just as *many* bedrooms as the apartment.
- Do you have as *many* friends as Jeniffer? 

2\) Put the words in order to form a sentence:

1) **chairs** - **kitchen** - **as** - **doesn't** - **room** - **have** - **the** - **living** - **as** - **many** - **The**

		- The kitchen doesn't have as many chairs as the the living room

2) **room** - **as** - **living** - **doesn't** - **balcony** - **The** - **as** - **the** - **light** - **have** - **much**

		- The balcony doesn't have as much light as the living room

3) **traffic** - **much** - **Blumenau** - **has** - **Joinville** - **as** - **as**

		- Joinville has as much traffic as Blumenau

4) **as** - **tourists** -**many** - **Floripa** - **have** - **doesn't** - **Joinville** - **as**

		- Joinville doesn't have as many tourists as Floripa

5) **have** - **doesn't** - **Rio** - **beaches** - **many** - **as** - **as** - **Floripa**

		- Floripa doesn't have as many beaches as Rio
